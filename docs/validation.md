## Controlled native observations

The primary used the same native diagnostic NSTextView, actual double-Shift events and animation enabled, ten confirmed attempts per version. Baseline release-to-completion observations were 211–249 ms; the first optimized snapshot was 147–186 ms, all 10/10 confirmed, and even reversals restored the initial word. Baseline screenshots took 23–33 ms. These small controlled observations show the gate/capture reduction worked in that fixture; they are not a broad performance benchmark or a p95 estimate. The production app itself requires 20 confirmed samples in the same editor/mode before showing median/p95.

The callback includes small post-acknowledgement cleanup; the trace ends at editor acknowledgement and excludes reveal. The target app's visible rendered frame has not been measured.

The final core repeated the same10 native word conversions with animation: callback times were160,174,155,175,149,151,174,177,176,151ms (median167ms, range149–177ms).
